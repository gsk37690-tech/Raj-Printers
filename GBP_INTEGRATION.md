# Google Business Profile (GBP) Integration Guide

This guide explains how to properly link and integrate the website with **Raj Printers'** Google Business Profile to maximize local ranking and trust without violating Google's guidelines.

---

## Step-by-Step Integration Checklist

- [ ] **Step 1: Publish Website to Live Domain**
  - Ensure the website is published on an HTTPS domain (e.g., `https://rajprinters.in`).

- [ ] **Step 2: Verify NAP Consistency (Name, Address, Phone)**
  - Ensure the business name on the profile is exactly `Raj Printers`.
  - Ensure the address matches: `94, Kariyamanickam Road, Siva Complex, Samayapuram, Tamil Nadu 621112`.
  - Ensure phone numbers match: `070105 64303` / `93427 09489`.

- [ ] **Step 3: Add Website URL to Google Business Profile**
  - Sign in to the Google Account that manages the business profile.
  - Navigate to **Edit Profile** > **Contact** > **Website**.
  - Add your live website URL (e.g., `https://rajprinters.in`).

- [ ] **Step 4: Add Business Services to GBP**
  - In GBP, go to **Edit Services**.
  - Select the primary category: **Print shop**.
  - Add specific service items:
    - *Visiting Card Printing*
    - *Flex & Poster Printing*
    - *Wedding Invitation Printing*
    - *Flyers & Pamphlet Printing*
    - *Bill Books & Commercial Printing*
    - *Xerox & Lamination*

- [ ] **Step 5: Upload High Quality Real Shop Photos**
  - Go to **Add Photo** in GBP.
  - Upload 3-5 interior photos of printing machines.
  - Upload 2-3 exterior photos showing the shop front signage at Siva Complex.
  - Upload 5-10 sample photos of printed visiting cards, flex banners, and invitations.

- [ ] **Step 6: Maintain Business Hours**
  - Set opening hours to match: **Monday - Saturday: 8:00 AM – 10:00 PM**.

- [ ] **Step 7: Collect Genuine Customer Reviews**
  - Share the direct review link (`https://maps.app.goo.gl/1FNJHGQRjnb7kzYv6`) with satisfied customers.
  - Respond politely to all customer reviews on Google Maps.

---

## Google Policy Compliance Guidelines
- **Never buy fake reviews** or use automated review tools.
- **Never stuff keywords** into your business title (do not rename to "Raj Printers Best Flex Banner Printing Shop Trichy").
- **Never create multiple listings** for the same physical location.
